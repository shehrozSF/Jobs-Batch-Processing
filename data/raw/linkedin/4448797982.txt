About Aiden Risk
Aiden Risk is the first AI-native commercial insurance brokerage—not a software layer on top of a traditional agency, but a fundamentally new operating model. Our AI handles submissions, renewals, endorsements, certificates of insurance (COIs), and coverage analysis at near-zero marginal cost across every commercial line and industry vertical.
What We're Building
The insurance industry is a $500B market still running on the same playbook it used in 1980. There are 39,000 independent agencies, it costs approximately $560 to service a single policy, and expertise doesn't scale beyond the people in the room. We're changing that.
Our proprietary AI platform handles submissions, renewals, endorsements, certificates of insurance (COIs), coverage analysis, and client communication across every commercial line and industry vertical—at near-zero marginal cost. The result is a single agency with unlimited specialization and a cost structure that no traditional brokerage can match.
