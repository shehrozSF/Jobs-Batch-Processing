About NCR Atleos
NCR Atleos, headquartered in Atlanta, is a leader in expanding financial access. Our dedicated 20,000 employees optimize the branch, improve operational efficiency and maximize self-service availability for financial institutions and retailers across the globe.
Title: Software Engineer
Location: Karachi, Pakistan
Job Summary
We are seeking a motivated and detail-oriented Software Engineer with 2 to 3 years of professional experience in software development. The ideal candidate will have hands-on experience in Microsoft .NET technologies (C#, ASP.NET, .NET Core/.NET) and Python for automation, integrations, data processing, and backend development.
Key Areas Of Responsibility
- Design, develop, test, and maintain enterprise applications using Microsoft .NET technologies and Python.
- Build and enhance web applications, APIs, and backend services using C#, ASP.NET, and .NET Core/.NET.
- Develop Python-based solutions to support automation, system integrations, data processing, and backend functionality.
- Collaborate with business stakeholders and cross-functional teams to deliver high-quality, scalable, and reliable software solutions that support business objectives.
- Participate in all phases of the software development lifecycle, including design, development, testing, deployment, and support.
- Write clean, maintainable, and efficient code in accordance with established coding standards and best practices.
- Troubleshoot, debug, and resolve application issues to ensure optimal system performance and reliability.
- Perform unit testing and support integration and user acceptance testing activities.
- Maintain technical documentation and contribute to continuous improvement initiatives within the development team.
- Work with databases, APIs, and integrated systems to support business and operational needs.
Basic Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in computer science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field.
- Minimum of 2 years of hands-on experience with Microsoft .NET technologies, including C#, ASP.NET, and .NET Core/.NET.
- Minimum of 2 years of hands-on experience using Python for automation, integrations, data processing, backend development, or related software engineering activities.
- Experience developing, testing, and maintaining enterprise applications.
- Strong understanding of object-oriented programming principles and software development best practices.
- Experience working with APIs, databases, and system integrations.
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, and troubleshooting skills.
- Ability to work effectively in a collaborative and cross-functional team environment.
- Strong verbal and written communication skills.
