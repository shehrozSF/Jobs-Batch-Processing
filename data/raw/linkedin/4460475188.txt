Senior AI Full-Stack Engineer
📍 Location : CoLabs, Gulberg II, Lahore | On-site
🕒 Type : Full-Time | 📈 Experience : 4+ years
🏢 Team : Lahore Engineering Hub
About VGA
VGA is a Kuwait-based technology company building AI-driven products across e-commerce, gaming, AI, and SaaS for clients throughout the GCC and beyond. Our Lahore engineering hub at CoLabs, Gulberg II is where we design and ship high-quality digital products — from interactive gaming and fantasy-sports experiences to scalable enterprise platforms.
We're at the stage where shipping fast isn't enough on its own — we want to change how software gets built. That's where you come in: designing the automated, agent-driven pipeline that turns requirements into production-ready software.
The Role
We're hiring a Senior AI Engineer to design and ship production-grade agentic AI systems — RAG pipelines, multi-agent workflows, voicebots, and chatbots — across e-commerce and SaaS verticals.
You'll own backend services end-to-end: design, build, deploy, monitor, iterate. This is a hands-on engineering role, not a research seat. You'll work directly with founders and senior engineers, make architecture calls, and see your work in front of paying customers within weeks.
What You'll Do
- Design and build agentic systems with LangChain and LangGraph — orchestration, tool use, memory, and planning
- Build RAG pipelines end-to-end: chunking strategies, embeddings, vector stores, retrieval evals, and re-ranking
- Develop backend services in Python using FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, and Prisma
- Ship voicebots, chatbots, and agent-as-a-service products integrated with client systems
- Build integrations with Shopify, Amazon Marketplace, and Salla for catalog, orders, and automation workflows
- Own LLMOps: prompt versioning, evals, tracing, guardrails, and cost/latency monitoring
- Design and optimize SQL schemas for high-throughput workloads
- Deploy on AWS (ECS/EKS, Lambda, RDS, S3, SQS, Bedrock) with CI/CD, IaC, and observability baked in
- Review code, mentor mid-level engineers, and drive architecture decisions
What We're Looking For
- BS in Computer Science, AI, Data Science, or a related field
- 4+ years shipping production AI and/or backend systems
- Expert-level Python, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, and SQL (schema design, query optimization, indexing)
- Production experience with LangChain, LangGraph, LLMs, multi-agent systems, and RAG
- Track record of building and deploying voicebots, chatbots, or agent-as-a-service products in production
- Hands-on e-commerce integration experience with Shopify, Amazon Marketplace, or Salla
- AWS proficiency — deploying and operating services in production, not just toy projects
- DevOps fundamentals: Docker, CI/CD, observability
- Vector DB experience (pgvector, Pinecone, Weaviate, or Qdrant)
- A backend specialist mindset — you think in services, contracts, latency budgets, and failure modes
Nice to Have
- Django or Flask experience
- Kubernetes in production
- Real-time voice infrastructure (Twilio, LiveKit, Deepgram, ElevenLabs), streaming inference, WebSockets
- Fine-tuning, distillation, or model serving (vLLM, TGI, SageMaker)
- Open-source contributions in the AI / agents ecosystem
Why VGA
- Real ownership — you'll lead systems, not tickets
- Senior team, flat structure — direct collaboration with founders and lead engineers
- Production at scale — your work runs in front of paying GCC customers from day one
- Modern stack — LangGraph, FastAPI, AWS Bedrock, pgvector; no legacy baggage
- Competitive compensation — base salary + performance bonuses, commensurate with experience
- Premium workspace — CoLabs Gulberg II, one of Lahore's best engineering environments
