Job Summary
Plutus21 is hiring a Senior AI Systems Engineer to architect the data and AI platform that powers our internal operations, client integrations, and quantitative-research tooling. You will own the design of scalable, well-governed data systems that ingest and process high volumes of financial and operational documents, and build the retrieval, agentic, and automation layers on top of them. This is an architecture-owning role that partners with engineering, operations, and the partner group.
Key Responsibilties:
- Architect and own the firm's data platform: warehousing, schema and data modeling, ETL/ELT pipelines, and data governance across internal and client workloads.
- Design and build production RAG and agentic AI systems — vector storage, embeddings, retrieval pipelines, and multi-agent workflows — for document processing, extraction, classification, and validation against source records at scale.
- Build scalable, high-concurrency backend services and orchestration for high-volume data processing.
- Design and operate infrastructure across cloud (AWS and/or GCP) with Terraform/IaC, CI/CD, containerization, monitoring, and cost optimization.
- Identify and replace manual, high-volume, error-prone workflows with reliable, well-documented automated systems, with appropriate controls, error handling, and human-in-the-loop review.
- Implement authentication, authorization, data access controls, and security best practices for sensitive financial data.
- Set technical direction, conduct code and design reviews, and mentor engineers.
- Partner directly with stakeholders and the leadership group to scope problems and execute independently. Required Qualifications
- 4+ years of software/data engineering, with demonstrated ownership of data architecture — data warehousing, schema/data modeling, ETL/ELT pipelines, and data governance. (BigQuery, dbt, Airflow, or equivalent.)
- Proven experience designing production RAG / retrieval / agentic AI systems : vector databases, embeddings, LangChain/LangGraph or equivalent, and LLM APIs (OpenAI/Anthropic/others).
- Strong backend engineering in Python (FastAPI/Django or equivalent) and high-volume, high-concurrency system design.
- Multi-cloud experience (AWS and/or GCP) with Terraform/IaC , CI/CD, and containerization (Docker/Kubernetes).
- Fluency across relational and non-relational databases and data-pipeline design.
- Sound judgment on where automation is appropriate and where human review must remain; strong attention to detail; high integrity with sensitive/confidential data.
- Ability to interpret a business problem, engage stakeholders, and lead delivery with minimal direction. Nice to have
- MS in Machine Learning, Computer Science, or a related field; strong CS fundamentals (system design, data structures).
- Experience in financial services, investment management, or multi-entity/data-governance-heavy environments.
- Open-source contributions, published work, or a portfolio of production AI/data systems.
- Experience across distributed/international teams.
