About The Job
Join our Team - Your career journey starts here – not just a job, but a future.
Our Potential Opportunity
INTECH Automation Intelligence is seeking a Senior Full Stack Developer to join our dynamic Industrial Intelligence team. INTECH IIS applications are built on its proprietary Industry 4.0 platform. They help address operational, optimization, and performance challenges faced by industries, be it in manufacturing, oil & gas, chemical & process, food & beverages, or metals & mining. These applications harness the capabilities of artificial intelligence, machine learning, physical and knowledge modeling, and visualizations for analyses on real-time and historical big data.
What You’ll Lead and Deliver:
- Design, develop, and maintain web applications using React for front-end development and Python for back-end services.
- Build new use cases and mini-apps from conceptualization to deployment, collaborating closely with product managers and UX/UI designers.
- Implement RESTful APIs, integrate third-party services, and manage server-side logic efficiently.
- Write clean, maintainable, and well-documented code while following best practices and coding standards.
- Optimize applications for maximum speed, scalability, and responsiveness.
- Conduct code reviews, unit testing, and ensure high-quality software delivery.
- Troubleshoot, debug, and enhance existing applications to improve functionality and user experience.
- Participate in architecture and design discussions, providing technical input on new features and solutions.
- Stay up-to-date with emerging technologies, frameworks, and best practices in software development.
Requirements:
To Be Successful in This Role, You Must Have:
- Proven experience of 3 - 5 years as a Full Stack Developer with strong React and Python skills.
- Hands-on experience in building web applications, APIs, and mini apps.
- Solid understanding of back-end development with Python, including frameworks such as FastAPI & Flask.
- Familiarity with relational and NoSQL databases, cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP), and version control systems (Git).
- Experience in developing responsive, scalable, and secure web applications.
- Excellent problem-solving skills and attention to detail.
- Strong communication skills and ability to work effectively in a collaborative team environment.
