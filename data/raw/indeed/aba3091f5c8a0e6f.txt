Our company develops internal analytics systems and automation tools for managing procurement, sales, logistics, and customer request processing.
The company builds enterprise platforms that help employees make data driven decisions, monitor key business KPIs, and automate operational workflows. These solutions integrate with ERP, CRM, and other internal systems and are used daily by sales, procurement, and operations teams.

We are looking for a second Python engineer to join the internal systems team. You will own specific functional areas and work alongside the lead developer.
Core focus areas include analytics dashboards, internal business applications, and automation tools. In addition to building new features, you will work with internal users to clarify requirements, maintain existing solutions, and help roll out new tools.
A key expectation is the ability to take a business problem all the way to a working solution, understand the business context, and operate at the intersection of engineering, analytics, and enterprise systems.

- Develop new functionality and fix defects in existing systems

- Clarify requirements and business logic for tasks

- Analyze and resolve issues in internal applications
- Participate in rolling out new features and services
