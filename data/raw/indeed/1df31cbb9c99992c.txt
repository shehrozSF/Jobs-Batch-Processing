About Southeast Culvert
Southeast Culvert Inc. operates on the biblical principle of being intentional about doing good. Our success is grounded in doing good for our customers, employees, suppliers, and the company itself by living out our core values.
Southeast Culvert was founded in 1981 on the promise to provide a quality drainage product at a reasonable price, when and where it is needed. This remains the core principle of how we run the business. Today, Southeast Culvert is the largest single site capacity culvert plant in the United States. We offer stormwater solutions for every need, and we are active members of PPI, NCSPA, and ASCE. In 2025, Southeast Culvert was named a mid-sized Engineering Employer of the Month. We support our community through contributions to schools, fire departments, churches, and the Shriners Children's Hospitals. Our mission is to build lasting relationships that better serve our customers and our community.

Work Structure and Development Cadence
This role is supported and advised by senior technical guidance and includes the following structure:
-
Regular code evaluation sessions. Working through real code, covering boundary conditions, error paths, silent failures, resource lifecycle, concurrency assumptions, and the difference between a test that proves something and one that restates the code.
-
Code reviews. Systematic reviews, tapering deliberately as capability develops.
-
Plan reviews. Review approach before implementation and code is written.
-
Explanation gates. Ensures the developer can explain the code before merging new code.
