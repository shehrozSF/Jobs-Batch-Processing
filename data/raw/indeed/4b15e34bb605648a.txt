We are looking for a Python / AI engineer to design, build, and maintain a suite of autonomous AI agents that automate customer communications and back-office tasks. You will also be responsible for helping design a cost-effective hosting, inference strategy, and selecting the right models, providers, and infrastructure to keep our AI operations both powerful and affordable.

- Develop and maintain agent pipelines: intent classification, entity extraction, context management, action execution, and human hand-off.
- Integrate AI agents with the existing Laravel backend via APIs, queues, and webhooks.
- Select, fine-tune, and evaluate LLMs for specific tasks — balancing capability, latency, and cost.
- Design and implement the cloud hosting and inference infrastructure.
- Monitor agent performance, track costs, and iterate on prompts and model choices to improve quality and reduce spend.

- Understanding of cost optimisation for LLM inference — token budgeting, caching, batching, and model selection.
- Comfortable working with async Python, REST APIs, and message queues.
- Strong problem-solving ability and a pragmatic approach to "good enough" AI. Nice To Have
