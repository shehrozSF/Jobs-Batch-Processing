Full-Stack Developer, Flutter & Backend (Remote/Faisalabad-Lahore)
Job Type: Full-time | Onsite (Pakistan, Faisalabad)
About the Role
Horti Club, one of Pakistan's largest horticulture and landscaping companies, is building a nationwide plants marketplace, a purpose-built delivery platform for plants, connecting customers with nurseries across the country, the idea is similar to Food Panda. We're looking for a strong full-stack developer who can own both the backend and the mobile app for this build from the ground up, working directly alongside our lead developer.
This is a real, funded, ground-floor project, not a maintenance role. You'll be one of two developers building the entire system: customer app, delivery logistics, nursery onboarding, and order management, end to end.
Responsibilities
· Build the backend (FastAPI, Python) powering orders, payments, nursery listings, and delivery logistics.
· Design and manage the database (PostgreSQL), including location-based logic for matching customers to nearby nurseries.
· Develop the customer-facing and delivery-rider mobile apps using Flutter, for both Android and iOS.
· Build and consume REST APIs connecting the mobile app, website, and backend.
· Work with mapping/location services and delivery-route logic.
· Publish and maintain apps on the Google Play Store and Apple App Store.
· Use Git/GitHub for version control, branching, and code review.
· Write clean, well-documented, maintainable code and collaborate closely with the rest of the team.
· Debug, test, and optimize for performance and reliability under real order volume.
Education
· At least a bachelors in software engineering or any related field.
Experience
· Minimum 3 years of real software engineering experience building scalable and easy to maintain systems.
Requirements
· Solid experience with Flutter for cross-platform mobile development and deploying apps to play store and apple store.
· Solid backend experience, FastAPI or a comparable Python framework.
· Strong working knowledge of PostgreSQL or similar relational databases.
· Comfortable owning a feature end to end: database, API, and mobile UI.
· Experience with REST API design and integration.
· Good understanding of Git/GitHub workflows.
· Experience publishing apps to the Google Play Store (App Store experience a strong plus).
· Experience deploying backends and databases.
· Strong problem-solving skills and ability to work independently with minimal hand-holding.
· Good communication skills, comfortable working closely with a small, fast-moving team.
Nice to Have
· Experience with location-based / geospatial features (maps, delivery radius, PostGIS).
· Experience with route optimization or logistics-style systems.
· Familiarity with payment gateway integrations (JazzCash, EasyPaisa, or similar).
· Experience with state management in Flutter (Bloc, Provider, Riverpod).
· Prior experience on a marketplace, delivery, or logistics app.
What We Offer
· Full-time, funded, high-ownership role on a major national project from day one.
· Work directly with leadership. Fast decisions, no bureaucracy.
· Competitive pay, based on experience.
· Real opportunity to grow with the project.
