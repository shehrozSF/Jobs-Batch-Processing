QA Automation Engineer (Tosca)
Contract-to-Hire | Hybrid | Birmingham AL
We are seeking a skilled QA Automation Engineer with strong Tosca automation experience to join a growing enterprise technology team. This is a contract-to-hire opportunity with an expected conversion timeline of approximately six months.
In this role, you will support enterprise-wide quality assurance initiatives, automation testing efforts, and test strategy development while helping drive the organization's automation maturity.
Responsibilities
-
Design, develop, and execute automated test solutions using Tosca
-
Create and maintain automated test scripts for enterprise applications
-
Develop comprehensive test plans, test cases, and testing strategies
-
Collaborate with development, QA, and business teams to ensure software quality
-
Support functional, regression, integration, and end-to-end testing efforts
-
Identify, document, and track software defects
-
Participate in automation framework enhancements and continuous improvement initiatives
-
Contribute to overall QA best practices and testing standards
Required Qualifications
-
Strong hands-on experience with Tosca Automation
-
Experience with Selenium
-
Java development experience
-
Background in both manual QA testing and automation testing
-
Ability to clearly articulate automation design, testing methodologies, and QA best practices
-
Strong communication and collaboration skills
Preferred Qualifications
-
Tosca Certifications (AS1, AS2, and/or DI)
-
Experience working in large enterprise environments
-
Bachelor's degree (required for permanent conversion)
Location & Schedule
-
Hybrid work schedule
-
Onsite: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
-
Remote: Monday and Friday
-
Local candidates preferred
-
Relocation candidates will be considered if able to relocate within 4–6 weeks
-
Initial onsite presence is important to support knowledge transfer and training
What We're Looking For
-
Well-spoken and professional
-
Direct and concise communicators
-
Strong technically with the ability to explain their experience clearly
-
Hands-on automation engineers who can demonstrate deep Tosca expertise
If you have a passion for test automation and are looking for an opportunity to make an impact within a highly visible enterprise environment, we'd love to connect.
