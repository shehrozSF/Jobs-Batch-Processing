This is a full-time, on-site opportunity for an entry-level Electronics Engineer based in Karachi. The role is ideal for fresh graduates or candidates with limited experience who are eager to develop their skills in electronic security and environmental monitoring systems. The position involves hands-on work with installation, commissioning, troubleshooting, and maintenance of various electronic systems. The work schedule follows a morning shift and includes some travel requirements. The offered salary range is PKR 30,000 to 500,000, reflecting potential growth based on experience and performance.
Key Responsibilities
- Install, commission, and maintain electronic security systems and environmental gas monitoring equipment.
- Troubleshoot and resolve technical issues related to electronic systems to ensure optimal performance.
- Prepare and maintain detailed technical documentation for all systems and processes.
- Provide technical support and assistance to customers, addressing their queries and concerns promptly.
- Coordinate effectively with suppliers to ensure timely procurement and delivery of necessary components and equipment.
- Participate in site visits and travel as required to support installation and maintenance activities.
Required Qualifications
- Bachelor’s degree in Electronics Engineering or a closely related field.
- Basic understanding of electronic security systems and environmental monitoring technologies.
- Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to troubleshoot electronic equipment.
- Good communication skills to interact with customers and suppliers professionally.
- Willingness to work on-site and travel as part of job responsibilities.
- Ability to prepare clear and concise technical documentation.
Preferred Qualifications and Benefits
- Prior internship or practical experience in electronics installation or maintenance is a plus.
- Familiarity with environmental gas monitoring systems will be advantageous.
- Opportunity to work in a dynamic environment with exposure to advanced electronic security technologies.
- Competitive salary package with room for growth based on performance and experience.
- Hands-on experience and professional development in a growing industry sector.
This role offers a valuable starting point for recent graduates aiming to build a career in electronics engineering with a focus on security and environmental systems. The candidate will gain practical experience and develop technical expertise while contributing to important safety and monitoring projects..
