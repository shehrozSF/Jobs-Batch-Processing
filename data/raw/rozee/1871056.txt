Ascend BPO Services is seeking a detail-oriented Junior Workforce Management Analyst to join our operations team. This role focuses on supporting workforce planning, scheduling, and reporting tasks to ensure efficient resource allocation and optimal service delivery. The ideal candidate holds a Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Quality and Performance Management, Project Management, Information Technology, or a related field, with 6 months to 2 years of experience in operation, project coordination, or administration roles. The candidate demonstrates strong time management skills and Excel proficiency, including formulas and pivot tables, to analyze data and support forecasting efforts.

The Junior Workforce Management Analyst works under supervision and does not manage a team. This position requires the ability to coordinate multiple tasks and collaborate across departments to maintain workforce schedules that align with business needs. Strong analytical capabilities and familiarity with workforce management and reporting tools are essential to contribute effectively in a fast-paced environment. The role emphasizes accuracy, proactive communication, and a commitment to continuous improvement in workforce and operational performance.
