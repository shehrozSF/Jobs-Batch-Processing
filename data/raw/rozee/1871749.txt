The Research Analyst at Poverty Eradication Initiative (PEI) plays a crucial role in supporting the organization’s mission by conducting thorough research and analysis to inform decision-making processes. This position involves gathering, organizing, and interpreting data using qualitative and quantitative methods to deliver clear and comprehensive insights. The analyst supports multiple stages of data collection and analysis, including documentary review and evidence indexing, to ensure data accuracy and relevance. With a focus on competitive and market research, the role demands strong report writing skills to clearly communicate findings and support program evaluations.

The Research Analyst collaborates closely with various stakeholders to maintain evidence logs and conduct data extraction and tabulation tasks during evaluation cycles. This role requires attention to detail and organizational skills to manage multiple preparation cycles and provide enhanced support during evaluation weeks. Although this position does not involve managing a team, it requires independent work and the ability to contribute meaningfully to multidisciplinary projects and strategic research efforts, supporting PEI’s goal of developing effective strategies for poverty eradication.
