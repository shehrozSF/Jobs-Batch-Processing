They will serve as a vital link between the company and potential clients, ensuring customer acquisition and satisfaction through skillful negotiation and understanding of client requirements. This role demands excellent communication skills, perseverance, and the ability to manage multiple leads in a fast-paced environment aimed at expanding the client base and driving company growth.
