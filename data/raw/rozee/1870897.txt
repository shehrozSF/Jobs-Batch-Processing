Myukhub is seeking a skilled Digital Product & Growth Coordinator / Business Analyst with at least three years of relevant experience to join our team. This role focuses on coordinating digital product initiatives and driving growth strategies through thorough business analysis. The successful candidate will play a vital part in gathering, analysing, and documenting business requirements to support our digital projects, collaborating closely with website developers, software teams, and external technology partners. The role demands strong project coordination abilities, commercial insight, and excellent stakeholder communication skills. Although this position does not involve managing a team, it requires working independently and delivering high-quality outputs reliably and efficiently.

In this role, you will prepare clear requirements documents, user stories, process maps, and acceptance criteria while maintaining comprehensive oversight of product roadmaps and growth strategies. You will contribute to digital product success by understanding key digital environments such as customer portals and content management systems, ensuring seamless integrations, and helping shape user experience research findings into actionable initiatives. Your confidence in communicating directly with senior stakeholders and your ability to manage multiple priorities will be essential in driving projects to completion. Proficiency in common project management tools, spreadsheets, and presentations is critical, along with responsible use of AI tools for research and analysis.
