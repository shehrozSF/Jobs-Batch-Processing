Aldo International Pvt. Ltd. is seeking an fresh Engineer to develop and maintain strong customer relationships while leveraging technical expertise to present and demonstrate products effectively. The Sales Engineer plays a crucial role in bridging the gap between technical teams and clients by understanding customer needs and delivering tailored technical solutions that align with those requirements. This position focuses on combining in-depth knowledge of products with excellent sales skills to drive revenue growth and enhance customer satisfaction. The ideal candidate collaborates closely with clients and internal teams to provide technical support during the sales cycle, ensuring all technical questions are addressed thoroughly. They contribute to the creation of proposals, technical presentations, and product demonstrations that highlight the advantages and specifications of the company's offerings. This role requires strong communication skills, the ability to explain complex technical concepts in simple terms, and a customer-focused attitude. The Sales Engineer does not manage a team but works alongside sales professionals and product specialists to achieve company objectives.
