Hd Staffing Solution is seeking a detail-oriented Estimation Engineer with at least one year of experience to join our team. The role does not involve managing a team but demands strong individual responsibility, attention to detail, and effective organizational skills to support project delivery and contribute to the overall success of Hd Staffing Solution’s projects.
