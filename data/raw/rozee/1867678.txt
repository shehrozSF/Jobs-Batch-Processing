Fulcrum Pvt Ltd is seeking a skilled and dedicated Technical Support Engineer / Technician with 3 years of experience to join their team. This role requires hands-on expertise in installing, troubleshooting, and maintaining software-based industrial equipment. The candidate will handle the commissioning and servicing of industrial machinery at various customer locations, ensuring smooth equipment operation and high customer satisfaction. The position involves frequent travel to customer sites and requires excellent problem-solving abilities and strong IT knowledge. Additionally, the selected individual will lead and manage a small team of one person, providing guidance and support in technical tasks and service delivery.

The ideal candidate holds a diploma or bachelor's degree in a relevant technical discipline such as Electronics, Electrical, Mechanical, Mechatronics, Computer Engineering, or Information Technology. With a commitment to delivering timely and effective service, this role combines technical expertise with customer service and team leadership. Candidates with their own motorcycle for site visits will have an added advantage. Working hours are Monday to Saturday, from 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM, and the role is based on-site in Karachi.
