Isekai Digital is seeking a motivated Junior Full Stack Developer to join their dynamic development team. In this role, you will collaborate closely with senior developers, designers, and project managers to develop and maintain websites, web applications, and digital products tailored for our clients. This position provides a unique opportunity for a developer with 1 to 2 years of practical experience or a strong portfolio to deepen their skills in both frontend and backend technologies while embracing AI-assisted development tools.

As a Junior Full Stack Developer, you will independently handle coding tasks at a junior level and incorporate AI-based coding assistants to enhance research, debugging, and development speed. Curiosity, adaptability, and a keen desire to learn new technologies quickly are essential. Although you will not be leading a team, collaboration and communication with team members are vital for project success. The role encourages a strong understanding of coding practices, debugging, and problem-solving with an emphasis on responsible use of AI to improve productivity and code quality.
