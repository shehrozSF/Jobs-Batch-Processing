This full-time remote position based in Karachi, Pakistan, offers an opportunity to work as a Credit Risk Analyst supporting investment products and fund risk management. The role focuses on ensuring accurate portfolio risk reporting, validating data, and maintaining monitoring tools primarily using Excel. The analyst will also be responsible for covenant and early-warning monitoring, conducting risk analysis, maintaining audit trails, and contributing to process improvements within the team. This position requires a minimum of three years of relevant experience in financial analysis, credit risk, investment operations, portfolio monitoring, reporting, banking, or related fields.
Key Responsibilities
- Prepare and deliver portfolio risk reports to support investment and fund risk management activities.
- Validate and ensure the accuracy of financial and risk data used in reporting and analysis.
- Develop and maintain Excel-based monitoring tools to track portfolio performance and risk indicators.
- Monitor covenants and early-warning signals to identify potential credit risk issues proactively.
- Conduct detailed credit risk analysis to assess the impact of various risk factors on investment portfolios.
- Maintain comprehensive audit trails for all risk-related activities to ensure compliance and transparency.
- Identify opportunities for process improvements and implement changes to enhance efficiency and accuracy in risk management workflows.
Required Qualifications
- A minimum of three years of professional experience in financial analysis, credit risk, investment operations, portfolio monitoring, reporting, banking, or a closely related discipline.
- Strong proficiency in Microsoft Excel, including the ability to create and manage complex monitoring tools and reports.
- Solid understanding of credit risk concepts, portfolio risk management, and investment product dynamics.
- Experience with data validation techniques and maintaining audit trails in a financial or risk management environment.
- Analytical mindset with attention to detail and the ability to work independently in a remote setting.
- Excellent communication skills to effectively collaborate with cross-functional teams and present risk findings clearly.
Preferred Qualifications and Benefits
While not explicitly stated, candidates with additional experience in fund risk management or investment product support will have an advantage. The remote nature of the role offers flexibility and the opportunity to work within a dynamic financial services environment based in Karachi. This position provides a platform for professional growth in credit risk analysis and investment risk management.
This role is ideal for professionals seeking to leverage their financial and credit risk expertise in a remote capacity, contributing to robust risk management practices within an investment-focused organization..
