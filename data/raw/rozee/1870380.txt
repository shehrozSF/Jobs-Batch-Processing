Prime BPO 5 is seeking dedicated and motivated Customer Engagement Specialists to join its outbound US sales campaigns. This role is designed for both fresh graduates and experienced call center agents aiming to enhance their careers and achieve uncapped earning potential. The ideal candidate actively listens to customer needs, promotes products and services effectively, and works towards consistently meeting daily and monthly sales targets. Candidates must be comfortable working night shifts and have a commitment to maintaining professional call quality and strict compliance with call center standards.

This role requires excellent verbal communication skills and a strong sales aptitude. The Customer Engagement Specialist works independently within a structured environment, ensuring customer satisfaction while driving revenue growth for Prime BPO 5. The company offers a competitive salary structure complemented by performance-based commissions, paid annual leaves, and opportunities for career advancement. Candidates should be willing to work during night hours and must not be students or currently employed elsewhere. This position does not include team management responsibilities but focuses heavily on individual performance and proactive customer engagement.
