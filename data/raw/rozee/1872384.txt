- Collaborate closely with the design team to understand and refine game concepts and specifications.
- Develop effective strategies and establish streamlined development pipelines to optimize workflow.

- Translate creative design ideas into fully functional and polished game elements.
- Write, test, and maintain efficient, clean, and well-documented game code.
- Identify and troubleshoot code errors and performance issues to ensure smooth gameplay.
- Conduct comprehensive testing of game mechanics and thematic components to maintain quality.
- Ensure all game products adhere to high industry standards for performance and user experience.
- Design and develop game patches and updates to enhance gameplay and resolve bugs.

- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related technical field.
- Proven experience as a Unity or Unity3D developer with a strong portfolio of completed projects.
- Proficiency in Unity and Unity3D platforms, including scripting and game physics integration.
- Solid understanding of 3D game development principles and physics engines.
- Experience developing games for both console and mobile platforms.
- Excellent communication skills to collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams.
- Knowledge of memory management, space optimization, and performance tuning techniques.
- Familiarity with scripting languages, animation workflows, session management, and texture application.
- Strong problem-solving skills with the ability to diagnose and resolve technical issues efficiently.
- Exceptional attention to detail to ensure high-quality and polished game development.
