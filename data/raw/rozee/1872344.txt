Innovex Enterprises is seeking a dedicated Amazon Virtual Assistant with a minimum of five years of experience to support our growing e-commerce operations. This role focuses on managing various aspects of Amazon Seller Central to enhance product listings, optimize sales strategies, and maintain inventory. The ideal candidate will have practical experience with Amazon Seller Central, strong skills in keyword and product research, and an ability to handle PPC campaign management and account health monitoring. This position requires a proactive individual capable of problem-solving and meeting deadlines, with the added responsibility of managing a team of two persons to ensure seamless day-to-day operations.

The Amazon Virtual Assistant will play a crucial role in improving our online sales presence by handling product listing creation and optimization, keyword research using specialized tools, and inventory tracking with appropriate software. Additionally, managing customer reviews and conducting in-depth sales reporting will be essential duties. The successful candidate will maintain effective communication both within the team and with external contacts to resolve issues promptly and maintain smooth workflows. A reliable internet connection is necessary to perform remote duties efficiently. This role demands a detail-oriented professional who takes ownership of assigned tasks while driving the team towards meeting company growth objectives.
