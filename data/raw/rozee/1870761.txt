Khan Group of Construction seeks a skilled Civil Engineer to contribute to the design, planning, and supervision of construction projects. The ideal candidate possesses strong expertise in civil engineering design and demonstrates effective communication skills. This role focuses on applying engineering principles to develop and maintain safe, functional, and sustainable infrastructure with attention to detail and adherence to industry standards. The position does not involve managing a team but requires collaboration with various stakeholders to ensure project success.

The Civil Engineer will engage in site inspections, analysis, and coordination with architects, contractors, and local authorities. The role demands precise documentation, problem-solving, and commitment to quality assurance throughout the project lifecycle. The candidate must stay current with engineering codes and emerging construction technologies to support innovative solutions within the company’s portfolio.
