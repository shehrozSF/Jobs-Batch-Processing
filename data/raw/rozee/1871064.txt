Isekai Digital is seeking a highly skilled Senior Full Stack Developer with at least 4 years of professional experience to lead and manage a team of 3 developers. The ideal candidate will take technical ownership of end-to-end project lifecycles, from initial planning through deployment, ensuring the delivery of robust, scalable, and secure web applications and digital platforms. This role requires designing and implementing both frontend and backend systems while working closely with UI/UX designers, project managers, and account managers to translate business and product requirements into effective technical solutions. The Senior Full Stack Developer will provide mentorship, conduct code reviews, and uphold development standards, driving technical excellence across projects. Effective troubleshooting and optimization of performance, security, and scalability are critical components of this position.

In this role, the developer will lead the integration and development of APIs, databases, and third-party services, managing deployments and version control using industry-standard tools and practices. The successful candidate demonstrates strong expertise in system architecture design and DevOps practices, fosters continuous integration and deployment, and implements automated testing to maintain high-quality codebases. By conducting technical reviews and researching new technologies, the Senior Full Stack Developer will innovate and enhance project capabilities within Isekai Digital, contributing significantly to the company’s mission of delivering value-driven digital solutions.
