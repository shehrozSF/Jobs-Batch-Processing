Humayun Wellchem Trading Concern is seeking an experienced Quality Control Analyst to join our Quality Control Laboratory located at Sundar Industrial Estate, Lahore, Pakistan. This role requires a dedicated professional with a master's degree in Pharmacy and a minimum of 10 years hands-on experience in the Basic and Semi-Basic Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient (API) manufacturing industry. The ideal candidate will have strong expertise in analytical testing, compliance with current Good Manufacturing Practices (cGMP), and regulatory standards including ICH, USP, BP, EP, and DRAP. The role includes working with advanced analytical instruments and leading a small team of three professionals to maintain high-quality standards in our laboratory testing procedures and documentation.
