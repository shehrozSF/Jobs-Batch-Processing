Location: Islamabad
Compensation: PKR 30,000–50,000/month
Position: Technical Project Lead
About the Opportunity
We are looking for a highly motivated and capable individual to take on a Technical Project Lead position within a growing professional team.
This is a selective opportunity for someone who wants to move beyond simply completing development tasks and take genuine responsibility for technology, project execution, team coordination and product development .
The successful candidate will work closely with senior leadership and will have the opportunity to build and lead a team of developers, interns and trainees.
Key Responsibilities
- Lead day-to-day technical development activities
- Translate requirements into clear technical tasks
- Plan development milestones and project timelines
- Coordinate developers, interns and trainees
- Review code and development work
- Maintain GitHub repositories and development workflows
- Help establish appropriate technical standards and processes
- Identify technical issues and provide practical solutions
- Participate in technical hiring and candidate evaluation
- Mentor junior developers and students
- Monitor project progress and ensure deadlines are maintained
- Prepare regular progress updates for senior leadership
- Contribute to technical architecture and technology decisions
- Ensure development work is properly documented and organized Candidate Profile
We are looking for someone with a strong technical foundation who also demonstrates leadership potential .
Preferred qualifications
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience in Computer Science, Software Engineering, IT or a related field
- Strong understanding of modern web development
- Experience with one or more modern development frameworks
- Familiarity with React/Next.js, Node.js, Python, Laravel or comparable technologies
- Understanding of databases, APIs and software architecture
- Strong Git/GitHub knowledge
- Experience developing and deploying applications
- Ability to independently troubleshoot technical problems
- Strong communication and organizational skills
- Ability to mentor and coordinate junior developers Previous experience leading:
- university projects
- freelance development teams
- internships
- software projects
- student developers will be considered a strong advantage.
What Makes This Opportunity Different
The position offers the opportunity to develop both technical and leadership experience .
The selected candidate will have the opportunity to:
- Work directly with senior leadership
- Take ownership of important technical decisions
- Build and manage a development team
- Mentor upcoming developers
- Gain hands-on technical leadership experience
- Participate in recruitment and team building
- Develop experience across product development and project management
- Build a strong professional portfolio
- Progress into increasingly senior responsibilities based on performance High-performing individuals may be considered for expanded responsibilities, increased compensation and long-term career opportunities as the organization develops.

Ownership — takes responsibility rather than waiting for instructions.
