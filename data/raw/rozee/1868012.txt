The Sales Engineer at PROCESS CONTROL SOLUTIONS plays a crucial role in driving revenue growth by promoting and selling industrial automation products, measurement devices, and control systems to clients in commercial and manufacturing sectors. This entry-level position is ideal for fresh graduates or candidates with basic product knowledge in industrial automation, especially those with educational backgrounds in BBA, DAE Instrumentation, or Electrical and Instrumentation Engineering. The Sales Engineer is responsible for identifying new business opportunities, providing technical consultations to clients, and developing comprehensive proposals and quotations that meet customer needs. This role requires frequent travel to client sites, thus candidates must possess a valid driving license and a personal bike, with fuel expenses covered separately. The Sales Engineer does not manage a team but collaborates closely with internal engineering, application teams, and vendors to ensure seamless order execution and after-sales service. Performance incentives supplement the basic salary, rewarding successful sales initiatives and customer relationship building. Strong negotiation skills and the ability to manage client relationships are key to succeeding in this role.
