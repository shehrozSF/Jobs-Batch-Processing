Aminova Technologies is seeking a highly motivated Business Developer Officer - Freelance Specialist to join our dynamic team remotely. The ideal candidate will have hands-on experience with leading freelance platforms such as Upwork, Fiverr, Guru, and Freelancer.com, specializing in securing software and IT projects. This role focuses on driving business growth by effectively managing company profiles, submitting compelling bids, and engaging clients to establish fruitful, long-term partnerships. Candidates should demonstrate a strong understanding of software development services, including web, mobile, artificial intelligence, and cloud technologies. This position suits individuals who are self-driven, capable of working independently, and committed to meeting monthly revenue goals without managing a team.

In this role, you will act as a key contributor to Aminova Technologies’ global expansion efforts by executing strategic outreach on freelance platforms. Your responsibilities will include daily bidding, customizing proposals to convert prospects, profile optimization, and maintaining exceptional client satisfaction standards. By tracking performance metrics and adapting strategies accordingly, you will support the company’s ambition to win competitive IT projects worldwide. This opportunity offers a flexible remote work environment that values initiative and results-driven efforts in freelance business development.
