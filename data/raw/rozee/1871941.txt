Zetech Solutions is looking for a skilled WordPress Developer to join our on-site team in Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Block 3, Karachi. This full-time position involves building, customizing, and maintaining responsive WordPress websites that align with client requirements. The ideal candidate will have practical experience with WordPress and Elementor, strong problem-solving abilities, and the capacity to deliver high-quality websites within set deadlines. The role offers a competitive monthly salary between PKR 40,000 and 60,000, with working hours from Monday to Saturday, 6:00 PM to 3:00 AM.
Key Responsibilities
- Develop and customize WordPress websites according to project specifications
- Use Elementor and other page builders to design engaging and user-friendly layouts
- Customize themes and plugins to enhance website functionality and user experience
- Troubleshoot and resolve website errors and layout issues efficiently
- Create mobile-friendly, responsive web pages to ensure optimal performance across devices
- Improve website speed, security, and overall performance
- Manage regular updates and ongoing website maintenance tasks
Required Qualifications
- Proven hands-on experience with WordPress and Elementor page builder
- Basic knowledge of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
- Ability to translate design concepts into fully functional websites
- Strong understanding of responsive web design principles
- Excellent problem-solving skills and keen attention to detail
- Ability to manage multiple tasks and meet deadlines effectively
- A portfolio showcasing previous WordPress projects is mandatory
Additional Information
Candidates shortlisted for this role will be required to complete a practical WordPress test as part of the selection process. Applicants should submit their CV along with a link to their website design portfolio. Those who have previously applied for WordPress-related positions at Zetech Solutions are requested not to reapply.
