Texas Lab Technologies is looking for a highly skilled Senior Graphic Designer & UI/UX Developer with at least eight years of professional experience in graphic design and user interface development. This full-time remote role requires an expert-level proficiency in Figma and a strong command of Adobe Creative Suite tools including Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, and Lightroom. The successful candidate will create visually compelling graphics and design engaging, user-friendly digital interfaces for various web applications, websites, and platforms.

As a key member of the creative team, the Senior Graphic Designer & UI/UX Developer will collaborate closely with development and marketing departments to produce innovative and responsive designs that enhance brand identity and improve overall user engagement. The role demands a creative professional who is detail-oriented, highly organized, and capable of delivering high-quality visual and interactive design work aligned with modern web standards and best practices.
