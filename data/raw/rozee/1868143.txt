C&B Marketing is seeking a detail-oriented and experienced Accountant to manage and oversee our financial activities. This role requires a strong background in accounting principles, financial reporting, and reconciliation within the fast-moving consumer goods industry. The Accountant will be responsible for maintaining accurate financial records and ensuring compliance with accounting standards. The position requires at least eight years of experience and includes supervising a dedicated team of three accounting professionals to support financial operations.

The ideal candidate will possess excellent analytical abilities and communication skills, with proficiency in MS Excel and various accounting software. This role demands consistent coordination of audits, managing general ledger activities, and preparing comprehensive financial statements. By applying rigorous attention to detail and expert knowledge, the Accountant will contribute to the financial stability and integrity at C&B Marketing.
