Blinks International is seeking a detail-oriented Product Research Analyst to join our team. The ideal candidate possesses a strong foundation in market analysis and consumer behavior, with the ability to conduct comprehensive product lifecycle assessments. This role requires expertise in competitor research and product identification to support brand development initiatives. The Product Research Analyst will gather and analyze market data to provide actionable insights that influence product strategies and business decisions. This position offers an opportunity to work in a dynamic environment and contribute significantly to the company’s growth and market presence.

The successful candidate will collaborate cross-functionally with various departments to ensure that product offerings meet market demands and align with brand vision. This role demands accurate and timely research outputs using advanced market research tools and methods. Effective communication and presentation of findings to stakeholders will be a routine responsibility. While this position does not involve managing a team, it requires independent work with strong analytical thinking and problem-solving capabilities to impact product development positively.
